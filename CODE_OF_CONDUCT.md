# Community code of conduct

Ultimate Assistant's community should be useful, curious, and safe to participate in.

Be respectful. Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people. Give
constructive feedback, respect privacy, credit others' work, and make room for
people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
misinformation, doxxing, and publishing another person's credentials or private
AI conversations are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove content,
close contributions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the
community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub
profile contact channel. Include links or screenshots when safe, but do not
redistribute sensitive material. Reports will be reviewed as privately and
fairly as possible; immediate safety concerns should go to the appropriate
local authority or platform safety team.
