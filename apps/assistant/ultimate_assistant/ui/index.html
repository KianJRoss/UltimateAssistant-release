g4f accounts and browser sessions
          
G4F is a gray area: it reaches providers through their websites rather than their official APIs, which can go against a provider's terms. A provider may limit, block or ban an account used this way, and we aren't responsible if that happens. Use it at your own risk.

          

            
            
            
            
            
          

          
          
Each account has its own local g4f process. Provider support and session requirements vary; a saved session is unverified until a provider request succeeds.

          

          
Protected capture supports ChatGPT. Sign in to the intended ChatGPT tab, connect its Kapture extension, start capture here, then send a short message in that tab. Other g4f providers can use their native g4f UI or a local session export.
